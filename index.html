How it works

  1. Connect your speakers. Pair a Bluetooth speaker to any phone or laptop in its Settings, or just use the device's own speaker. Each device plays through whatever it's connected to.
  2. Start a room on one device and add songs. Everyone else scans the QR code or types the code.
  3. Press play. Every device plays in step. Tap Auto-sync once and SpeakerSync listens with a microphone to cancel out each speaker's Bluetooth delay.
